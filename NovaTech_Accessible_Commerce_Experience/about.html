About NovaTech

Five students. One shared idea.

We combined web development, design, and business thinking to create a simple, responsible e-commerce experience.

Our mission

Make useful technology easier to choose.

NovaTech presents reliable and affordable accessories in a focused catalog. We believe clear information, honest interactions, and a straightforward design help customers make better decisions.

Our story

Built from a shared frustration.

Our group was tired of sorting through unclear listings for cables, audio gear, chargers, and cases. We created the kind of store we would want to use: fast to browse, simple to search, and transparent about being a classroom demonstration.
