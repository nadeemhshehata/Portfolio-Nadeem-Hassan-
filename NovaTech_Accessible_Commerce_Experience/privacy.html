Privacy at NovaTech

Clear choices, minimal data.

This policy describes the student demonstration as it works today—not a future commercial store.

◉

Your privacy control

NovaTech saves only your demo cart in this browser. Clear it at any time.

Last updated: September 28, 2026

1. About this website

NovaTech is an academic e-commerce demonstration. It does not create accounts, process real orders, accept payments, or operate a customer database.

2. Information you enter

The newsletter field demonstrates form validation, but the email address is not transmitted or stored. The checkout button is also a demonstration and never asks for payment, shipping, or billing information.

3. Browser storage

When you add an item to the cart, the product and quantity are stored in your browser’s local storage under the key novatech_cart. This keeps the cart available as you move between pages. The NovaTech team cannot access this local copy.

4. Cookies and tracking

The site does not set advertising cookies, use analytics trackers, or build visitor profiles. It is self-contained and does not load third-party fonts or product images.

5. Hosting information

The public site may be hosted by GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may process standard technical request information needed to deliver and secure the website. That processing is governed by GitHub’s own privacy practices.

6. How to control your data

Use the “Clear saved cart” button on this page to remove NovaTech’s local storage entry. You can also clear site data through your browser settings. Removing it resets the cart badge and order summary.

7. Data security

Collecting no customer or payment data substantially reduces privacy risk. If the site becomes a real store, secure payment processing, access controls, retention limits, consent management, and a revised policy would be required before launch.

8. Children’s privacy

This demonstration is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

9. Policy changes

If the site’s features or data practices change, this page should be updated at the same time. The revision date above shows when this policy was last reviewed.

10. Contact

Questions about this classroom project can be sent to privacy@novatech.example. This example address is included for demonstration and is not monitored.
